# ChatChat Product Principles

## 1. The user proposes once

The user starts the meeting with one proposal. Later consultation rounds are automatic unless the user explicitly chooses to intervene in a future feature.

## 2. AI participants are equal peers

No chair. No king. No delegation. No provider receives extra authority because it is popular, first, faster or repeated across tabs.

## 3. Independent before shared

Round 1 is sealed. Every participant forms an initial view without seeing peer outputs.

## 4. Shared context is structured

Claims, challenges, evidence, support, revisions and concessions become typed events instead of disappearing inside one long transcript.

## 5. Changing your mind is progress

A visible revision after better evidence is one of ChatChat's most important moments.

## 6. Majority is not truth

Alignment is reported, but majority pressure must not be treated as proof.

## 7. Disagreement survives

Different final positions are part of the result, not noise to hide.

## 8. Browser first

The primary product is the browser extension / web experience. It reuses users' existing signed-in AI tabs and asks for Provider-site access only when the user chooses to attach that AI.

## 9. Local-first, not magically offline

ChatChat has no relay server. Online AI providers still receive the prompts/context sent to their websites from the user's browser.

## 10. International from the start

English and Simplified Chinese are first-class UI languages. The protocol remains language-neutral.

## 11. The interface may be playful; the protocol must be sober

Animations, changed-mind moments and influence maps can be delightful. They must be derived from real structured events rather than invented drama.
